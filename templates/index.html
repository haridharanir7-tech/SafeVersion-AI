What Safe Vision AI Can Analyze

The prototype demonstrates how an intelligent content-safety platform can classify different types of potentially harmful digital media.

01 / VISUAL SAFETY

Explicit Content

Identify potentially inappropriate or explicit visual content and determine whether the content should be allowed, reviewed, or blocked.

02 / CONTENT RISK

Violence Detection

Prototype workflow for identifying potentially violent, graphic, or disturbing visual material.

03 / MEDIA AUTHENTICITY

Deepfake Analysis

Prototype interface for identifying potentially manipulated, synthetic, or AI-generated media.

04 / ONLINE SAFETY

Scam Detection

Analyze suspicious digital content and highlight potential scam, phishing, or deceptive-content risks.

05 / SOCIAL SAFETY

Cyberbullying Awareness

Prototype concept for identifying toxic, threatening, or potentially harmful online content.

06 / USER PROTECTION

Harmful Content Awareness

Help users understand potentially harmful digital material through risk scores and explainable results.

How Safe Vision AI Works

A simple workflow connects the user interface with the AI analysis layer.

01
Upload User selects digital content.
02
Analyze Content is sent to the AI backend.
03
Classify AI evaluates the content.
04
Score Risk Confidence and risk are calculated.
05
Protect A recommended safety action is shown.

Privacy-First Architecture

Safe Vision AI is designed with privacy and responsible content analysis in mind.

Responsible AI

Analysis results should be presented as confidence-based predictions rather than absolute truth. Users should be able to understand why content has been flagged.
